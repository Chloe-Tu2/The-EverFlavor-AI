# Governance

EverFlavor AI is built by Team EverGlow (ITAI 2277 Capstone). The team decides together; changes reach `main`
through pull requests that pass every check.

- **Who approves what:** [docs/governance/human_approval_workflow.md](docs/governance/human_approval_workflow.md)
  (safety rules and allergen words need two people; releases, licences and new datasets need one).
- **What may be public:** [docs/governance/public_data_policy.md](docs/governance/public_data_policy.md).
- **Licences:** [docs/governance/license_compliance.md](docs/governance/license_compliance.md) and
  [docs/sources.md](docs/sources.md).
- **Releases:** [docs/governance/release_checklist.md](docs/governance/release_checklist.md).
- **Contributing:** [docs/CONTRIBUTING.md](docs/CONTRIBUTING.md). **Security:** [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Open questions:** [docs/governance/needs_human_review.md](docs/governance/needs_human_review.md).

AI assistants work under the same rules: they may audit, report, fix their own work and open pull requests; they
stop and write the question down when a licence, an origin, an allergen or personal data is unclear; and they
merge only where a person has said so in writing.
