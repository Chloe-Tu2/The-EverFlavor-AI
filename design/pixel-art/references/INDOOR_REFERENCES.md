# Real kitchens: references for the next art pass

The traced kitchens (`../drafts/traced-kitchens/`) follow the team's concept art closely. This page is for the next
step: making the rooms and the objects in them read as real kitchens. The changes are kept small, a few per kitchen;
everything else stays as the team drew it. Each change can be reviewed on its own, object by object.

All photos are on Wikimedia Commons, under the license shown. They are references only: look, then draw. Nothing
from them is copied into the art or the repository. If a photo is ever used directly, its license needs credit (and
CC BY-SA asks for the same license on what is made from it).

## In every kitchen

- **One eye level.** Counters, shelves and the floor lines should meet at the same vanishing point, so the room
  reads as one space.
- **Real heights.** Counter about 90 cm, wall shelves about 150 cm and up, stools 45 to 65 cm. Jars, bottles and
  pans should be sized against the counter, not against each other.
- **Light from the windows.** The lighting pass already adds glow at the windows and lamps; shadows fall away from
  the window and under shelves and cabinets.
- **Somewhere to wash and somewhere to cook.** Every real kitchen has both; two of ours are missing one (below).
