`*_original.jpg` is the picture as the team made it. The files without `_original` have **the sign's text painted out**
(the letters filled in from the paper around them, with OpenCV's inpainting), because the app writes its own
title, "EverFlavor Kitchen", as a separate element: on a phone the picture is cropped and a title painted on it
would be lost.
