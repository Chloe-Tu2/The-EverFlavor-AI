# Lantern references

Photos of lanterns unlit by day and lit at night, for the kitchens' day and night versions (`../../day_night.py`,
`lamps_off`). What they show: an unlit lantern's glass is grey or dark and reflects the room, with no warm hue; an
unlit red paper lantern is a flat, matte red; lit, the glass or paper glows yellow-orange and lights the wall.

`lanterns_sheet.png` puts the photos next to our lanterns (afternoon, off | night, on).
