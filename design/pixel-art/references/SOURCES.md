# References and Sources

What the concept art draws on, and where art and photos can come from. Links only: nothing here is copied
from these games or sites, so no one else's art ends up in the repository.

## Style inspirations (look, do not copy)

| Reference | What to take from it |
|---|---|
| Stardew Valley (<https://www.stardewvalley.net/>) | A cozy farmhouse kitchen in warm pixel art; small readable objects on shelves |
| Cooking Mama (Nintendo DS series) | Cooking as a friendly game with a guide character who reacts to what you do |
| Overcooked (Team17) | Clear, chunky kitchen objects that read at a glance |
| Unpacking (Witch Beam) | Detailed pixel kitchens and shelves; how much a few pixels can say |
| A Short Hike (adamgryu) | Soft color palettes and light that changes with the time of day |

Real kitchens, for making the rooms and objects more accurate: [INDOOR_REFERENCES.md](INDOOR_REFERENCES.md)
(photos on Wikimedia Commons, with their licenses, and the few changes each kitchen needs).

## Palettes

- **Classic** is Eva's palette from the first themed app (`#FFF1E6` cream, `#8B4513` brown, `#FFB6C1` pink).
- The other themes were picked for this concept. More palettes made for pixel art:
  Lospec palette list, <https://lospec.com/palette-list>.

## Font

- **Jersey 10** (titles, tabs, buttons, numbers), Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License:
  <https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Jersey+10>.
- **VT323** (the chef's lines, small labels), Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License:
  <https://fonts.google.com/specimen/VT323>.
- Body text stays in a normal font for readability. Pixelify Sans was tried first and dropped: its B, C, 2 and 5
  were hard to read.
