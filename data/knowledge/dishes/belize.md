### Recado rojo

Recado is a culinary paste historically associated with Mayan cuisine.

- From: Belize
- Ingredients (Wikidata): Origanum vulgare, annatto, clove, cumin seed

Fun facts:

- (about) Recado is also an ingredient for a number of popular Latin dishes.
