### Pan de coco (Honduran cuisine)

Pan de coco, literally "coconut bread" in Spanish, is a dense, cake-like bread from the Garifuna people of the Caribbean coast located in Honduras.

- From: Honduras
- Origin (Wikidata): Honduras

Fun facts:

- (origin) It likely originated from the Caribbean, due to Antillean migration to the Bay Islands region of Honduras and along La Mosquitia between the 18th and 20th centuries.
- (origin) Pan de coco is similar to Belizean Creole bread and Jamaican coconut bread which are small, soft, round and savory, as well as, another variation called coco bread which also originated from the island.
- (origin) Pan de coco comes from Honduras.
