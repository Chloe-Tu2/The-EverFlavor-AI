### Torta Tre Monti

Torta Tre Monti is a traditional Sammarinese cake made of layers of thin waffled wafers adhered together by chocolate or hazelnut crème.

- From: San Marino
- Origin (Wikidata): San Marino
- Ingredients (Wikidata): wafer cookie
