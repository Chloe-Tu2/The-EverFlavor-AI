- Ingredients (Wikidata): chorizo, llama, blood sausage, pork, beef, lamb meat

### Locro

Locro or lojro (from the Quechua ruqru) is a hearty thick squash or potato stew, associated with Native Andean civilizations, and popular along the Andes mountain range.

- From: Argentina
- Ingredients (Wikidata): meat

Fun facts:

- (culture) Locro is one of the national dishes of Peru, Bolivia, Ecuador, Chile, Paraguay, Argentina and Southwestern Colombia.
- (culture) Locro is among the most important main foods representing Argentinan gastronomy, ranking as third among 28 different foods in a study regarding traditional Argentinan gastronomy.

### Pollo al disco

Pollo al disco is a hearty Argentine dish consisting of chicken and various vegetables cooked as a stew in an uncovered deep pan over an open fire. "Disco" refers to the pan used to cook the chicken, which is usually around 24 inches in diameter and at least 6 inches deep.

- From: Argentina
- Origin (Wikidata): Argentina

Fun facts:

- (origin) It is unknown where and when pollo al disco originated, but it is thought that the first pollo al disco was cooked using a disused plow disk in a rural area of Buenos Aires province.
- (about) Pollo al disco remains a popular meal for parties and other gatherings.
- (origin) Pollo al disco comes from Argentina.
