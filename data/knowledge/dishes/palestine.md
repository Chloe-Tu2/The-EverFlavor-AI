- Origin (Wikidata): Syria

Tamr hindi is a beverage consumed in Egypt and the Levant made from tamarind, water, and sugar.

- From: Palestine

Fun facts:

### Tamriyeh

Tamriyeh is a sweet made by enveloping a cream filling (typically semolina pudding) with filo dough then frying it, it is common in cities on the eastern Mediterranean like Nablus in the West Bank, Homs in Syria, Sidon in Lebanon, and Port Said in Egypt.

- From: Palestine
- Ingredients (Wikidata): sugar, dough, semolina pudding
