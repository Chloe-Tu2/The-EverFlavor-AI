### Ifisashi

Ifisashi (in Bemba) or visashi (in Chinyanja) is a traditional Zambian dish prepared with greens and peanuts.

- From: Zambia
- Origin (Wikidata): Zambia
- Ingredients (Wikidata): water, tomato, pumpkin, peanut oil, onion

Fun facts:

- (origin) Ifisashi comes from Zambia.

### Chikanda

Chikanda is a Zambian dish made from the boiled root tubers of terrestrial orchids also called chikanda.

- From: Zambia
- Origin (Wikidata): Zambia
- Ingredients (Wikidata): table salt, Habenaria, chili pepper, Disa

Fun facts:

- (culture) Chikanda is traditionally associated with the Bemba tribe in northeast Zambia, although it is eaten throughout Zambia today.
- (origin) Chikanda comes from Zambia.
- (culture) Now, chikanda can be found being sold by street vendors, supermarkets, and large restaurants as the urban population has an increased demand for this rural tradition.

### Thobwa

Thobwa is a Malawian and Zambian fermented drink that has a milky appearance, a cereal taste and a grainy texture.

- From: Zambia
- Origin (Wikidata): Zambia, Malawi
- Ingredients (Wikidata): millet, hot water, sorghum grain, maize flour

Fun facts:

- (about) Thobwa is made from white maize and millet or sorghum and is popular in all parts of the country.
- (origin) Thobwa comes from Zambia, Malawi.
