### Kuwaiti tea

Kuwaiti tea refers to several varieties of tea traditionally served in that country.

- From: Kuwait
- Origin (Wikidata): Kuwait

Fun facts:

- (culture) This tea is served with breakfast or during the traditional tea ceremony.
- (inspiration) The tea is created by putting water in a boiler together with cinnamon sticks and sugar.
- (origin) Kuwaiti tea comes from Kuwait.

More fun facts (when the user asks for more):

- (culture) Kuwaiti tea is a Kuwaiti tradition to offer a guest to a house a cup of tea or Arabic coffee.
