### Smažený sýr

Smažený sýr or vyprážaný syr – both meaning "fried cheese" – is a Czech and Slovak cheese-based dish that is widely consumed in both countries of the former state of Czechoslovakia.

- From: Czech Republic
- Origin (Wikidata): Czech Republic
- Ingredients (Wikidata): cheese

Fun facts:

- (about) A slice of cheese about 1.5 cm (0.6 in) thick is first breaded with flour, egg, and bread crumbs and then fried either in a pan or deep-fat fryer.
- (culture) Smažený sýr is a common street food in both countries and is popular among students as an inexpensive staple in school canteens.
- (origin) Smažený sýr comes from Czech Republic.

### Hermelín

### Vánočka

Vánočka is a plaited bread, baked in the Czech Republic and Slovakia (called vianočka in Slovak) traditionally around the Christmas holiday.

- From: Czech Republic
- Origin (Wikidata): Czech Republic
- Ingredients (Wikidata): egg as food

Fun facts:

- (history) A Christmas bread made from white flour, either in the form of a wedge or a plait, was first mentioned around 1400 by the Benedictine monk Jan of Holešov in his work Treatise on Christmas Eve.
- (inspiration) The bread is named after Vánoce, meaning 'Christmas' in Czech (Vianoce in Slovak).
- (culture) According to his interpretation, this pastry symbolized the Christ Child wrapped in cloth.

More fun facts (when the user asks for more):

- (history) Vánočka was further referred to during the 16th century, where it could only be made by a baker who was a guild craftsman.
- (history) During the 18th century, people took the recipe and began baking it at home.
- (origin) Vánočka comes from Czech Republic.
