### Caravane cheese

Caravane, also known as Camelbert, is the brand name of a camel milk cheese produced in Mauritania by Tiviski, a company founded by Nancy Abeiderrhamane in 1987.

- From: Mauritania
- Origin (Wikidata): Mauritania

Fun facts:

### Zrig

Zrig is a fermented milk beverage of the west Sahara.

- From: Mauritania

Fun facts:

- (about) Preparing the curdled milk to produce zrig is a woman's job; the first time a woman makes curdled milk for the family is a significant rite of passage (el-marwab).
- (culture) A traditional drink of Mauritanian, Senegalese and Western Saharan cuisine, zrig is made from soured milk mixed with sugar and cut with water; regional variants include additions of flavorings and grains.
- (history) In his travels in the mid-14th century, explorer Ibn Battuta documented that he was given a beverage of sour milk, millet and honey cut with water when he visited the town of Oualata, Mauritania.

More fun facts (when the user asks for more):

- (culture) Zrig is valued for being refreshing in the Saharan climate, and is traditionally consumed at the beginning of iftar during Ramadan.
- (culture) Zrig is traditionally drank from a gdah, a traditionally carved wooden bowl, or drank from a calabash jug directly.
- (culture) Zrig is a typical drink during iftar during Ramadan, to break the fast before drinking the traditional three cups of Maghrebi mint tea.
