### Apple cheese

Apple cheese is a traditional Lithuanian and Polish dessert, made out of boiled or cooked apples sweetened with sugar.

- From: Lithuania
- Origin (Wikidata): Poland, Lithuania

Fun facts:

- (origin) Apple cheese originated in the Middle Ages.
- (history) In 1907, the recipe for apple cheese was published in the Lithuanian magazine Lietuvos virėja from Lithuania's capital Vilnius.

### Kibinai

Kibinai, kybyn, or kibin (plural in Karaim: kybynlar / qıbınlar (Common Turkic Latin); singular in Lithuanian: kibinas) are traditional pastries filled with mutton and onion, popular with the Karaite ethnic minority in Lithuania.

- From: Lithuania
- Ingredients (Wikidata): onion, dough, mutton

Fun facts:

- (culture) The tradition of cooking them was inscribed on the National Register of Elements of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Ukraine in 2018.
- (about) These meat pies are also made by the Karaite minority of Melitopol in Ukraine, where they are known as et ayaklak.

Fun facts:

### Skilandis

Skilandis or Kindziukas is a Lithuanian matured sausage made of meat, fat, salt, pepper and garlic.

- From: Lithuania
- Origin (Wikidata): Lithuania

Fun facts:

- (history) Skilandis dates back to at least the 16th century - the word skilandis is referred to in documents from various locations across the Grand Duchy of Lithuania as early as in the 16th-18th centuries.
- (culture) The term Lietuviškas skilandis has been protected as a Traditional Speciality Guaranteed.
- (origin) Skilandis comes from Lithuania.

More fun facts (when the user asks for more):

- (culture) The ground meat is traditionally pressed into a pig's stomach or bladder, but today may be contained in other skins.
