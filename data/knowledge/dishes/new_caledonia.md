### Bougna

Bougna is a traditional feast dish of the Kanak people of New Caledonia.

- From: New Caledonia
- Origin (Wikidata): New Caledonia

Fun facts:

- (about) Bougna is also eaten in Vanuatu, where it is known as bunya or bunia.
- (origin) Bougna comes from New Caledonia.
