### Mofo gasy

Mofo Gasy, translated as "Malagasy bread", is a Malagasy traditional recipe typically eaten as a breakfast.

- From: Madagascar
- Origin (Wikidata): Madagascar
- Ingredients (Wikidata): rice

Fun facts:

- (origin) Mofo gasy is believed to originate from Asian and African culinary influences that have shaped Madagascar’s food culture.
- (culture) Traditionally sold by street vendors, it has become a staple part of daily Malagasy cuisine.
- (inspiration) Variations of the recipe occur based on the regions of Madagascar.

More fun facts (when the user asks for more):

- (origin) Mofo gasy comes from Madagascar.
- (culture) Its traditional preparation has been passed down through generations, although modern variations have emerged, including the addition of ingredients such as vanilla or coconut.

### Three Horses Beer

Three Horses Beer (better known locally as THB) is a pale lager that has been brewed by Star Breweries of Madagascar since 1958.

- From: Madagascar
- Origin (Wikidata): Madagascar
- Since: 1956-

Fun facts:

- (about) The Malagasy beer is produced at two breweries in Madagascar, the first centrally located in Antsirabe and the other in the northern city of Antsiranana.
- (history) In 1957, a brewer was recruited to develop a local beer.
- (culture) These have included sponsoring the THB Champions League, Madagascar's national football championship, and holding annual beer festivals.

More fun facts (when the user asks for more):

- (history) Three Horses Beer dates from 1956-.
- (history) The company first began producing Three Horses Beer (THB) in 1958.
- (origin) Three Horses Beer comes from Madagascar.
- (culture) In addition, THB is a regular sponsor of local musicians through major annual festivals and tours.
- (history) In 1968, Star Breweries opened a factory in Antsiranana to produce and distribute THB, Coca-Cola, and other carbonated beverages throughout the island's northern region.
- (history) In 1980, Star Breweries became partly state-owned; in 1989, Rochefortaise sold Star Breweries to Groupe Fraise, and the company obtained fully privatized status one year later.
- (about) THB is sold nationwide and, since 2005, has been exported to such markets as France, Reunion Island, Comoros, and Mayotte.
- (about) Recent investments in Star Brewery infrastructure have allowed a 20% increase in production since 2011.
- (about) In 2005, the production of THB in cans began at the Star brewery in Antsirabe.
- (about) In 2011 Groupe Fraise sold Star Breweries to French brewer Castel.
