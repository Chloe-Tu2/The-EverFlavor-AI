### Grogue

Grogue, also known as grogu or grogo (derived from English grog), is a Cape Verdean alcoholic beverage, an aguardente made from sugarcane.

- From: Cape Verde
- Origin (Wikidata): Cape Verde

Fun facts:

- (about) Grogue is the basis for a Cape Verdean cocktail known as ponche (derived from the English word "punch"), which also includes lime and molasses, comparable to the poncha of the island of Madeira.
- (culture) Grogue is made by an old traditional way and because of this there are a lot of different qualities on the market.
- (origin) Grogue comes from Cape Verde.

More fun facts (when the user asks for more):

- (about) The cane is processed in a press known as a trapiche.

### Strela (beer)

Strela is a beer that is produced by the company CERIS, based in the eastern part of Praia, Cape Verde.

- From: Cape Verde
- Since: 1996-

Fun facts:

- (history) Strela dates from 1996-.
- (about) In 2016, Strela beer received a Superior Taste Award from the International Taste & Quality Institute (iTQi) in Brussels.
- (about) In 2009, Strela was the second most consumed beer in Cape Verde, covering 35% of the market.
