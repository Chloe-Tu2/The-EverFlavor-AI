### Lahoh

Lahoh is a spongy, flat pancake-like bread.

- From: Somalia
- Origin (Wikidata): Somalia
- Ingredients (Wikidata): flour

Fun facts:

- (origin) Yemenite Jewish immigrants popularized the dish in Israel.
- (origin) Lahoh comes from Somalia.

### Sabaayad

Sabayad, also known as Kimis, Kibis or Ceesh, is a chewy, slightly flaky type of square Somali flatbread eaten in Somalia, Kenya, Ethiopia and Djibouti.

- From: Somalia
- Origin (Wikidata): Somalia
- Ingredients (Wikidata): flour

Fun facts:

### Bariis iskukaris

Bariis iskukaris, also called isku-dheh karis, is a traditional rice dish from Somali cuisine that is customarily cooked in a single pan or where the tenderization of both rice and stew occurs at some point in a single pan.

- From: Somalia
- Origin (Wikidata): Somalia
- Ingredients (Wikidata): raisin, Pisum sativum, Capsicum, chicken as food, basmati, onion

Fun facts:

- (origin) Bariis iskukaris comes from Somalia.
