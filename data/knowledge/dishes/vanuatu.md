### Tusker (Vanuatu beer)

Tusker is a lager produced in Port Vila, Vanuatu, by Vanuatu Brewing Ltd.

- From: Vanuatu

Fun facts:

- (about) Tusker is one of the most popular beers in the country and is often considered the national beer of Vanuatu.
- (culture) The name "Tusker" is a reference to the large tusks of a pig, a traditional symbol of strength, prosperity and wealth in Vanuatu, which has also been used as a currency.
- (about) As of 2016, cans of Tusker were brewed in New Caledonia.

More fun facts (when the user asks for more):

- (about) Bottled Tusker has been brewed in Port Vila since the founding of the brewery, and a large brewery expansion was taking place in 2024.
- (about) In 2022, a promotional oversized Tusker beer bottle at a roundabout in central Port Vila was replaced with a bottle of sparkling water after complaints from the Vanuatu Christian Council.
