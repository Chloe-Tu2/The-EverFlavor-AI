### Mandazi

Mandazi is a form of fried bread that originated on the Swahili coast of East Africa.

- From: Tanzania
- Origin (Wikidata): Tanzania
- Ingredients (Wikidata): water

Fun facts:

- (about) Mandazi is also known as bofrot or puff-puff in Western African countries such as Ghana and Nigeria.
- (origin) Mandazi comes from Tanzania.
- (about) Mandazi is popular in the region, as it is convenient to make, can be eaten with almost any food or dips or as a snack by itself, and can be saved and reheated for later consumption.

### Ming'oko

Ming'oko () is a wild root that grows in Makonde Plateau, Tanzania.

- From: Tanzania
- Origin (Wikidata): Tanzania

Fun facts:

- (history) In a research by Marja-Liisa Swantz in 1966, the digging of Ming'oko from the soil is a laborious process and a danger to women whose work it is.
- (origin) Ming'oko comes from Tanzania.

### Chipsi mayai

Chipsi mayai (Swahili for "chips and eggs"), also known as zege, is the most popular street food found all over Tanzania.

- From: Tanzania
- Origin (Wikidata): Tanzania
- Ingredients (Wikidata): oil, egg as food, fries

Fun facts:

- (about) Chipsi mayai was invented in the streets of Dar es Salaam.
- (culture) Chipsi mayai is the unofficial national dish of the country.
- (origin) Chipsi mayai comes from Tanzania.
