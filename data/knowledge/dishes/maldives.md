### Garudhiya

Garudhiya is a clear fish broth.

- From: Maldives
- Origin (Wikidata): Maldives
- Ingredients (Wikidata): Thunnini

Fun facts:

- (culture) Garudhiya is one of the basic and traditional food items of Maldivian cuisine.
- (origin) Garudhiya comes from Maldives.
- (inspiration) The broth is based on tuna species found in the nation's ocean waters such as skipjack, yellowfin tuna (kanneli), little tunny (lațți), or frigate tuna.

### Rihaakuru

Rihaakuru is a tuna-based thick sauce.

- From: Maldives

Fun facts:

- (culture) Rihaakuru is a traditional dish of Maldivian cuisine, consumed almost daily in every household in Maldives and in Minicoy since ancient times.
- (origin) Maldivian travellers introduced Rihaakuru and Bondi to Sri Lanka (then Ceylon).
- (history) These terms were popular in Sri Lankan households until the latter part of the 20th century, when they gradually disappeared.
