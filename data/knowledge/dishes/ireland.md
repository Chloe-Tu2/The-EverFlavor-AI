### Irish stew

Irish stew or stobhach is a stew from Ireland that is traditionally made with root vegetables and lamb or mutton, but in modern times also it is commonly made with beef.

- From: Ireland
- Origin (Wikidata): Ireland
- Ingredients (Wikidata): lamb meat

Fun facts:

- (culture) Irish stew is considered a national dish of Ireland.
- (origin) Irish stew comes from Ireland.
- (culture) As in all traditional folk dishes, the exact recipe is not consistent from time to time or place to place.

More fun facts (when the user asks for more):

- (culture) Irish stew is a celebrated Irish dish, yet its composition is a matter of dispute.
- (culture) Purists maintain that the only acceptable and traditional ingredients are neck mutton chops or kid, potatoes, onions, and water.
- (culture) Irish stew is the product of a culinary tradition that relied almost exclusively on cooking over an open fire.

### Spice bag

A spice bag (or spicebag, spicy bag; Irish: mála spíosrach) is a fast food dish, popular in most of Ireland and inspired by Chinese cuisine.

- From: Ireland
- Origin (Wikidata): Ireland

Fun facts:

- (about) Available in Chinese takeaways and chip shops since the 2010s, the dish has developed something of a cult following, and a Facebook group created as a tribute to the dish has attracted over 17,000 members.
- (origin) Spice bag comes from Ireland.
- (about) It is often cited as a popular hangover cure.

More fun facts (when the user asks for more):

- (about) Spice bag was voted 'Ireland's Favourite Takeaway Dish' in the 2020 Just Eat National Takeaway Awards in the Republic of Ireland, while in 2021 Deliveroo Ireland started a petition to create a "National Spice Bag Day".
- (about) According to research by RTÉ Radio reporter Liam Geraghty, the dish was created in around 2006 as an off-the-menu, after-hours meal by staff at the Sunflower Chinese takeaway restaurant in the Orwell Shopping Centre in Templeogue, Dublin.
