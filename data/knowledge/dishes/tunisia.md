### Assidat zgougou

Assidat zgougou (Arabic: عصيدة الزقوقو, /ʕsˁiːdətzɡuːɡuː/), literally Aleppo nut cream, is a Tunisian dessert often adorned with decorative patterns of pine nuts, pistachios and hazelnuts.

- From: Tunisia
- Origin (Wikidata): Tunisia
- Ingredients (Wikidata): milk, cream, flour, Pinus halepensis, Asida

Fun facts:

- (history) Assidat Zgougou was born out of the 1864-1867 famine in Tunisia.
- (culture) Assidat zgougou is prepared to celebrate the Mawlid and is made out of Aleppo pine powder, flour, milk, concentrated milk, and sugar.
- (origin) Assidat zgougou comes from Tunisia.

### Fricasse

A fricasse is a savory fried yeasted bread often filled with tuna, hard-boiled egg, olives, harissa, preserved lemons, capers and mashed potato, with turmeric as a condiment.

- From: Tunisia
- Origin (Wikidata): Tunisia

Fun facts:

- (origin) Fricasse was first made in Maghreb.
- (about) Not to be confused with fricassee.
- (culture) They are usually purchased from traditional Tunisian food vendors.

More fun facts (when the user asks for more):

- (origin) Oral history claims that the recipe originates in 19th century Tunisia.
- (origin) Fricasse is also widely popular in Israel, where it was introduced by Tunisian Jews, and France.
- (origin) Fricasse comes from Tunisia.

### Lablabi

Lablabi or lablebi is a traditional Tunisian dish based on chickpeas in a thin garlic- and cumin-flavored broth, served over small pieces of stale crusty bread.

- From: Tunisia
- Origin (Wikidata): Tunisia
- Ingredients (Wikidata): chickpea

Fun facts:

- (about) Lablabi is particularly popular among young people as a late-night meal, often consumed to mitigate the effects of alcohol after a night out.
- (origin) Lablabi comes from Tunisia.
