### Beshbarmak

Beshbarmak is a meat, noodles, and onion broth dish in Central Asian cuisine.

- From: Kyrgyzstan
- Ingredients (Wikidata): meat, onion, noodle, lamb meat

Fun facts:

- (about) Beshbarmak is typically served on large communal platters, shared between several people, after shorpo, a first course of mutton broth served in bowls called kese.
- (culture) The construction of beshbarmak as a national dish dates to the Soviet literature having inherited the nomadic and "settler" dichotomy from the Russian Empire.
- (about) Beshbarmak is also known as naryn in Xinjiang, Uzbekistan, Kyrgyzstan and Kazakhstan, as turama in Karakalpakstan and Dagestan, as dograma in Turkmenistan, and as bişbarmaq or qullama in Bashkortostan and Tatarstan.

More fun facts (when the user asks for more):

- (culture) Beshbarmak, which means "five fingers" in Turkic languages, refers to the traditional practice of eating the dish with one's hands.
- (about) This name is believed to have emerged later, especially after Russian cultural and ethnographic observations of the nomadic peoples of Central Asia.

### Laghman (food)

Laghman, also spelled lagman, is a dish of meat, vegetables and pulled noodles from Uyghur cuisine.

- From: Kyrgyzstan

Fun facts:

- (culture) Laghman is well known in Kazakhstan and Kyrgyzstan, where it is considered a national dish of the local Uyghur and Dungan (Hui) ethnic minorities.
- (about) In Chinese, the noodle is known as latiaozi or banmian.
- (about) Not to be confused with lawspeaker.

### Orama (dish)

### Jarma (drink)

Jarma is a cold beverage made from ground grains found in the cuisine of Kyrgyzstan.

- From: Kyrgyzstan
- Origin (Wikidata): Kyrgyzstan

Fun facts:

- (about) Today it is sold in stores and from barrels on the street throughout Kyrgyzstan, and is a popular go-to chilled drink during the summer.
- (origin) Jarma comes from Kyrgyzstan.
- (about) A small-scale producer and vendor in Nooken District was charging 23 soms (about 0.40 USD) per litre of jarma in the summer of 2015.
