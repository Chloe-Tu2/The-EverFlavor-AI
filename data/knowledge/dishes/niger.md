### Dambou

Dambu or dambou is a dish native to the Zarma and Songhai of Southwestern Niger made from cereals and moringa.

- From: Niger
- Origin (Wikidata): Niger, Nigeria
- Ingredients (Wikidata): maize, table salt, couscous, moringa, cooking oil, onion

Fun facts:

- (origin) Dambou comes from Niger, Nigeria.
- (about) Not to be confused with Dambou.
