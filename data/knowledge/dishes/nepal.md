### Kinema

Kinema, also known as hongema and hokima, is a traditional food of the Limbu people now also prepared by the Kirat people of the Eastern Himalayas region: Eastern Nepal, and Darjeeling, Kalimpong and Sikkim regions of India.

- From: Nepal
- Origin (Wikidata): Nepal
- Ingredients (Wikidata): soybean, soy bean

Fun facts:

- (origin) According to microbiologist Jyoti Prakash Tamang, kinema is estimated to have originated around 600 BC to 100 AD during Kirat dynasty rule, introduced by Limbu people.
- (about) The word kinema is believed to be derived from the Limbu language kinama, where ki means fermented and nama means to smell.
- (culture) Kinema is a traditional food of the Limbu people.
