### Poi (dessert)

Poi is a traditional Samoan dessert made from mashing fruit into a smooth consistency and mixing in coconut milk and other flavourings.

- From: Samoa
- Origin (Wikidata): Samoa
- Ingredients (Wikidata): mango, banana, coconut milk

Fun facts:

- (origin) Poi comes from Samoa.

### Koko Samoa

Koko Samoa is a traditional Samoan hot chocolate drink made from ground cocoa beans.

- From: Samoa

Fun facts:

- (origin) The cocoa tree (Theobroma cacao) is not native to Samoa but was introduced to the islands in 1883 by German colonists who brought plants of the Criollo subspecies from British Ceylon (present-day Sri Lanka).
