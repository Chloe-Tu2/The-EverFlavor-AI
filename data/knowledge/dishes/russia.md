- From: Russia
- Origin (Wikidata): Soviet Union
- Ingredients (Wikidata): carrot, potato, egg, onion, egg as food, Beta vulgaris, mayonnaise, Clupea, Beta, fried and pickled herring

Fun facts:

- (about) Dressed herring salad is popular in Russia, Belarus and other countries such as Poland, Lithuania and Latvia,.

- (origin) Dressed herring comes from Soviet Union.

### Karelian pasty

Karelian pasties, Karelian pies or Karelian pirogs are traditional Finnish pasties or pirogs originating from the region of Karelia.

- From: Russia
- Origin (Wikidata): Finland, Russia
- Ingredients (Wikidata): rice, potato, table salt, Hordeum vulgare, Secale cereale, rice pudding, millet, smetana, Hordeum, Oryza

Fun facts:

- (origin) In the 19th century, first potato, and then buckwheat were introduced as fillings, and later, boiled rice and millet.
- (culture) The oldest traditional pasties usually had a rye crust, but the North Karelian and Ladoga Karelian variants also contained wheat to improve the quality of the crust.
- (about) Today, the most popular version has a thin rye crust with a filling of rice.

More fun facts (when the user asks for more):

- (culture) Karelian pasties have had traditional speciality guaranteed (TSG) status in Europe since 2003.
- (culture) This means that any producer not following the traditional recipe cannot call them karjalanpiirakka and instead, will have to call them riisipiirakka 'rice pasties', perunapiirakka 'potato pasties', etc., depending on the filling.
- (origin) Karelian pasty comes from Finland, Russia.
