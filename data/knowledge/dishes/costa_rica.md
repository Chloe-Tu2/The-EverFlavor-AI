### Casado

A casado is a Costa Rican meal with rice, black beans, plantains, salad, a tortilla, and an optional protein source such as chicken, beef, pork, fish, and other ingredients.

- From: Costa Rica
- Origin (Wikidata): Costa Rica
- Ingredients (Wikidata): salad, meat, Phaseolus vulgaris, tortilla

Fun facts:

- (origin) The term may have originated when restaurant customers asked to be treated as casados, since married men ate such meals at home.
- (origin) Casado comes from Costa Rica.

- Ingredients (Wikidata): cheese

Fun facts:

### Guaro (drink)

Guaro is a liquor made in many places in Latin America.

- From: Costa Rica
- Ingredients (Wikidata): sugarcane

Fun facts:

- (culture) Guaro is traditionally 60 proof or 30% alcohol, although recently 70 proof and 80 proof versions are produced.
- (about) Guaro is popular in Colombia, Costa Rica, Ecuador, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, Nicaragua, and Panama, although in many places the word "guaro" can refer to almost any liquor.

### Imperial (beer)

Imperial (Cerveza Imperial) is a Costa Rican lager, manufactured by the Florida Ice & Farm Company (FIFCO).

- From: Costa Rica
- Origin (Wikidata): Costa Rica
- Since: 1924-

Fun facts:

- (history) Imperial was first produced by the Ortega brewery in 1924 by Carl Walter Steinvorth, an important businessman & the first orthodontist in Central America.
- (origin) Imperial comes from Costa Rica.
- (history) Imperial dates from 1924-.

### Mahi-mahi
