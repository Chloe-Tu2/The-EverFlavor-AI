### Zigni

Zigni, kaih tsebhi or kai wat is a popular Eritrean and Ethiopian stew (tsebhi or wat) made from meat, tomatoes, red onions and Berbere spices.

- From: Eritrea

Fun facts:

- (culture) Zigni is considered to be the national dish of Eritrea.
- (culture) The traditional recipe can take as long as five to six hours to prepare.
- (culture) A traditional component of Eritrean cuisine is tsebhi, which are meat stews served with injera.
