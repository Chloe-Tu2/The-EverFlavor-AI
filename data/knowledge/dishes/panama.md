### Chicheme

Chicheme is a beverage consisting of fermented atole or cream, made from pounded corn.

- From: Panama
- Ingredients (Wikidata): corn kernel

Fun facts:

- (culture) Chicheme is traditional in some Latin American countries and regions such as the Caribbean coast of Costa Rica, Colombia (mainly in the municipality of Ciénaga de Oro in Córdoba), and Panama (mainly in the Chiriquí Province and La Chorrera in the Panama Oeste Province).

### Seco Herrerano

Seco Herrerano is a brand of aguardiente (sugarcane liquor) produced in Pesé, Panama.

- From: Panama
- Origin (Wikidata): Panama

Fun facts:

- (inspiration) Seco Herrerano was created by the Varela family in 1908.
- (about) Seco was a popular and affordable rum in Panama.
- (origin) Seco Herrerano comes from Panama.
