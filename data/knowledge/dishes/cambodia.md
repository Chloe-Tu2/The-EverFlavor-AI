### Prahok

Prahok is a salted and fermented fish paste (usually of mudfish) used in Cambodian cuisine as a seasoning or a condiment.

- From: Cambodia
- Origin (Wikidata): Cambodia
- Ingredients (Wikidata): salted fish, fermented fish

Fun facts:

- (origin) Prahok originated as a way of preserving fish during the time of the year when fresh fish was not available in abundant supply.
- (origin) Prahok comes from Cambodia.
