### Kalik

Kalik is a Bahamian brand of beer.

- From: The Bahamas
- Since: 1988-

Fun facts:

- (origin) Kalik was first introduced in 1992 as a limited edition brew to mark the 500th anniversary of Columbus’ landing in the new world.
- (inspiration) Kalik was designed by Heineken International in 1988, based on studies of the Bahamian market.
- (culture) According to the bottle label the name of Kalik is derived from sound of cowbells heard during the annual Bahamian festival of Junkanoo.

More fun facts (when the user asks for more):

- (origin) Kalik Light, a light beer with 4.5% alc./vol., was introduced in 1997 in response to consumers’ requests for a Bahamian light beer.
- (history) Kalik dates from 1988-.
- (origin) Kalik Lime was introduced in 2010, Kalik beer with a hint of citrus.
- (origin) Kalik Radler was introduced in May 2014.

### Old sour

Old Sour is a sauce, ingredient, and salad dressing used in the Bahamas and Key West, Florida.

- From: The Bahamas

Fun facts:

- (origin) Old Sour sauce originated in the Bahamas, and is most often prepared using Key lime juice, salt, and bird peppers.
- (about) Old Sour is most popularly made from an aged (fermented) mixture of Key lime juice, bird peppers, and salt.
- (culture) In traditional Key West cooking, Old Sour is used for a variety of dishes including seafood, fried foods, and Key lime pie.
