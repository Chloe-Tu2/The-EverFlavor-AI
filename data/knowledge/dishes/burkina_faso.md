### Zoomkoom

Zoomkoom is a fermented beverage from Burkina Faso and Northern Ghana with a sweet taste. "Zoom" means flour and “koom” means water in the Mooré language.

- From: Burkina Faso
- Origin (Wikidata): Ghana, Burkina Faso

Fun facts:

- (about) Zoomkoom is also known as "Zonkuon" in the Dagara language, where "zon" means flour and "kuon" means water, similar to that of the Mooré language.
- (culture) There are varieties of zoomkoom, the traditional and modernized one.
- (origin) Zoomkoom comes from Ghana, Burkina Faso.

More fun facts (when the user asks for more):

- (culture) The traditional beverage is brown in color and the modernized has a white appearance because millet is used in place of guinea corn and without shea butter.
