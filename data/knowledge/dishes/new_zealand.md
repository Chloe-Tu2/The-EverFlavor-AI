### Chocolate fish

A chocolate fish or choccy fish is a traditional confectionery item in New Zealand.

- From: New Zealand
- Origin (Wikidata): New Zealand

Fun facts:

- (culture) Variants of the traditional item exist; a common version is made of solid orange-flavoured milk chocolate.
- (origin) Chocolate fish comes from New Zealand.

### Kānga pirau

Kānga pirau (which translates literally from Māori as rotten corn), is a fermented maize (corn) porridge dish which is considered a delicacy by many Māori people of New Zealand.

- From: New Zealand
- Origin (Wikidata): New Zealand

Fun facts:

- (origin) Kānga pirau comes from New Zealand.

### Kānga waru

Kānga waru (waru means 'to scrape') is a type of pudding from New Zealand.

- From: New Zealand

Fun facts:

- (origin) The dessert originates from the Māori people and is closely associated with Māori cuisine.
