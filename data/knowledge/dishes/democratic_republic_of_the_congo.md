### Babute

Babute is an African dish, effectively a form of meatloaf, originating in the Babute region of the Congo containing ground beef, curry powder and apricots.

- From: Democratic Republic of the Congo
- Origin (Wikidata): Democratic Republic of the Congo
- Ingredients (Wikidata): curry powder, ground beef, apricot

Fun facts:

- (culture) Babute is considered similar to bobotie, the South African national dish.
- (origin) Babute comes from the Democratic Republic of the Congo.
