### Ema datshi

Ema datshi is a spicy Bhutanese stew made from hot chili peppers and cheese.

- From: Bhutan
- Origin (Wikidata): Bhutan
- Ingredients (Wikidata): chili pepper, Datshi

Fun facts:

- (culture) Ema datshi is among the most famous dishes in Bhutanese cuisine, recognized as the national dish of the country.
- (origin) The term "Ema datshi" originates from Dzongkha, the official language of Bhutan.
- (culture) Over the years, ema datshi, which has its roots in Tibetan culinary traditions, has undergone various changes to suit the preference of local Bhutanese taste.

### Datshi

Datshi is a traditional Bhutanese cottage cheese commonly produced from cow milk.

- From: Bhutan
- Origin (Wikidata): Bhutan
- Ingredients (Wikidata): yak milk

Fun facts:

- (about) To make Datshi, Dahi is first prepared from raw milk, the Dahi is then processed into Mar (artisanal butter) by churning in a special wooden container called Theki.
- (origin) Datshi comes from Bhutan.
- (about) For example: the famous Bhutanese cuisine Ema datshi uses Datshi as the cheese and hence the name.

More fun facts (when the user asks for more):

- (about) Matured Datshi, known as Zoetay, is also widely consumed in Bhutan.
- (about) In 2018, 74% of produced milk was utilized in the production of dairy products.
