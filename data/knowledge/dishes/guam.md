### Kalamai (dessert)

Kalamai is a traditional Chamorro corn and coconut pudding, sometimes referred to as coconut gelatin (though no gelatin is actually used).

- From: Guam
- Origin (Wikidata): Guam, Mariana Islands
- Ingredients (Wikidata): water, sugar, coconut milk, masa

Fun facts:

- (culture) The kalamai mixture, once thickened, is traditionally poured into a low rimmed tray to a half-inch thickness.
- (origin) Kalamai comes from Guam, Mariana Islands.

### Kelaguen

Kelaguen is a Chamorro dish from the Mariana Islands eaten as a side dish or as a main course.

- From: Guam
- Origin (Wikidata): Guam, Northern Mariana Islands

Fun facts:

- (origin) Kelaguen was introduced by Filipino settlers when the Marianas and the Philippines were both part of the Spanish East Indies.
- (origin) Kelaguen comes from Guam, Northern Mariana Islands.

### Fina'denne'

Fina'denne' (many alternate spellings, commonly finadene, fina'denni', or fina'dene) is a spicy, all-purpose condiment that is a staple of Chamorro cuisine.

- From: Guam

Fun facts:

- (history) Anthropologists visiting Guam in the early 20th century noted the frequent use of fina'denne' by Chamorros.
- (origin) Filipino immigrants during the Spanish period introduced the technique of tapping coconut trees and fermenting the sap to make tubâ vinegar.
