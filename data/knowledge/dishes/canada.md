### B.C. roll

The B.C. roll is a kind of sushi containing barbecued salmon and cucumber.

- From: Canada
- Origin (Wikidata): Canada
- Named after: British Columbia
- Ingredients (Wikidata): salmon, cucumber

Fun facts:

- (culture) The Vancouver-based Japanese chef Hidekazu Tojo created the B.C. roll in 1974 when he used salmon skin in place of the traditional anago (salt-water eel), which was difficult to obtain in the West Coast.
- (inspiration) B.C. roll is named after British Columbia.
- (origin) The name comes from the fact that British Columbia (B.C.) is famous for wild Pacific salmon.
