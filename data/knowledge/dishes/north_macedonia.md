### Ajvar

Ajvar is a condiment made principally from sweet bell peppers and eggplants.

- From: North Macedonia
- Ingredients (Wikidata): Capsicum

Fun facts:

- (history) Domestic ajvar, meaning "caviar", used to be a very popular dish in Belgrade homes and restaurants, but the domestic production of caviar became unsteady in the 1890s because of labor disputes.
- (culture) Depending on the capsaicin content in bell peppers and the amount of added chili peppers, it can be sweet (traditional), piquant (the most common), or very hot.
- (history) The relish became a popular side dish throughout Yugoslavia after World War II and remains popular in Southeast Europe.

More fun facts (when the user asks for more):

- (history) Before the 20th century, significant local production of caviar occurred on the Danube, with sturgeon swimming from the Black Sea up to Belgrade.

### Pastrmajlija

Pastrmajlija, also rendered as Pastrmalija, is a Macedonian bread pie made from dough, cheese, eggs and meat.

- From: North Macedonia
- Origin (Wikidata): North Macedonia
- Ingredients (Wikidata): dough

Fun facts:

- (culture) The city of Štip organizes an annual pastrmajlija festival called Štipska Pastrmalijada.
- (about) Pastrmajlija is popular in Štip, Veles, Sveti Nikole, Kratovo, and Negotino.
- (origin) Pastrmajlija comes from North Macedonia.

### Tavče gravče

- From: North Macedonia
- Origin (Wikidata): North Macedonia
- Ingredients (Wikidata): bean

Fun facts:

- (culture) Tavče gravče is considered the national dish of North Macedonia. "Tavče gravče Tetovo style" is a popular regional variation.
- (origin) Tavče gravče comes from North Macedonia.
- (culture) This meal is baked and served in a traditional unglazed earthenware pot.
