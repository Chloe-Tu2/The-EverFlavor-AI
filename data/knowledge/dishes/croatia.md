### Bajadera

Bajadera is a layered nougat with almonds and hazelnuts invented and produced commercially by the Croatian Kraš company.

- From: Croatia
- Origin (Wikidata): Croatia
- Ingredients (Wikidata): nougat

Fun facts:

- (history) Initially packaged as part of bonbonnieres, bajadera became a separate product in 1954.
- (about) Thus, it became the first Croatian dish to earn that label.
- (origin) Bajadera comes from Croatia.

More fun facts (when the user asks for more):

- (history) It earned the label Izvorno hrvatsko by the Croatian Chamber of Economy in 1997.
- (history) Initially, bajadera was packaged as part of bonbonnieres, but in 1954 it became a separate product.
- (history) In 2009, Kraš redesigned its bajadera packaging that was last changed in 1989.
- (history) The Croatian Chamber of Economy awarded it the Izvorno hrvatsko label in 1997, intended to convey originality and quality.
- (about) Bajadera is used for special occasions and is the most sold and most famous Croatian praline; it is also known worldwide.

### Rab cake

The Rab cake is a traditional Croatian cake which originates from the Adriatic island of Rab.

- From: Croatia
- Origin (Wikidata): Croatia
- Ingredients (Wikidata): Prunus dulcis

- (inspiration) According to legend, this cake was first served in 1177 to Pope Alexander III when he consecrated the Assumption Cathedral in Rab.

### Ožujsko

Ožujsko is a Croatian brand of lager beer (5%).

- From: Croatia
- Origin (Wikidata): Croatia
- Since: 1892-

Fun facts:

- (history) The production of Ožujsko beer started in early 20th century in Lobe brewery (the exact year of the start of production in the Lobe brewery is not known; the year 1892 on today's label indicates the year of establishment of the Zagrebačka pivovara and not the start of the production of Ožujsko beer).
- (origin) Ožujsko beer originates from the Lobe brewery from Nova Gradiška.
- (history) The Lobe brewery was founded by Dragutin Lobe (Požega 1833. - Nova Gradiška 1924.) after he moved from Požega to Nova Gradiška in 1851.

More fun facts (when the user asks for more):

- (history) Lobe brewery was shut down in 1943. and it's machines of the production plant were transferred to today's Zagrebačka pivovara.
- (history) Ožujsko dates from 1892-.
- (history) In the beginning, the brewery employed around 60 workers, and its successful operation led to its modernization and increase in production capacity in 1873. when Lobe brewery became the first steam-powered brewery in Croatia.
- (history) Over time, the management of the Lobe brewery was taken over by Dragutin's son Miroslav Lobe (1861. - 1939.), under whose leadership the brewery progressed even more.
- (origin) Ožujsko comes from Croatia.
- (history) At the beginning of the 20th century Lobe brewery begins to invest capital in the development of Zagrebačka dionička pivovara i tvornica slada (today Zagrebačka pivovara).
- (history) Unfortunately, due to the lack of recognition of the importance of the brewery and its heritage by the local authorities in the mid-20th century, almost every trace of the once magnificent Lobe brewery has been erased.
