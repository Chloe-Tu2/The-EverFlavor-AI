### Bichak

Bichak is a stuffed baked or fried pastry that comes in different forms (round, triangle, quadrangle) appetizer or meal similar to a turnover, served in Central Asia cuisines including Uzbek cuisine, Tajik cuisine, Afghan cuisine, and Middle Eastern cuisine, most notably in Moroccan cuisine.

- From: Tajikistan
- Origin (Wikidata): Tajikistan
- Ingredients (Wikidata): meat, onion, flour, vegetable oil

Fun facts:

- (about) Bichak is also popular because it can be prepared in large quantities.
- (origin) Bichak comes from Tajikistan.
- (culture) They are traditional for Rosh Hashanah and Sukkot.
