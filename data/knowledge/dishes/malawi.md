### Kondowole

Kondowole is a staple of Malawian cuisine that is eaten predominantly in the northern region of Malawi.

- From: Malawi
- Ingredients (Wikidata): water, cassava

Fun facts:

- (about) Kondowole is normally eaten with fish; fish in Malawi ranges from "utaka" and "chambo" (a famous fish from Lake Malawi).

### Usipa

Usipa (scientific name: Engraulicypris sardella) is a "small sardine-like fish that occurs in large shoals".

- From: Malawi
- Origin (Wikidata): Malawi, Mozambique

Fun facts:

- (origin) Usipa comes from Malawi, Mozambique.
