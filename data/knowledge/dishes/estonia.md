### Egg butter

Egg butter is a mixture of butter and chopped hard-boiled eggs.

- From: Estonia
- Origin (Wikidata): Finland, Estonia
- Ingredients (Wikidata): butter, hard-boiled egg

Fun facts:

- (culture) In Estonia, egg butter and leib (dark rye bread) are traditionally included in the Easter Sunday meal.
- (origin) Egg butter comes from Finland, Estonia.

### Mulgipuder

Mulgipuder (also Mulgi puder) is pieces of potatoes mixed with pearl barley and topped with fried pork.

- From: Estonia
- Origin (Wikidata): Estonia
- Ingredients (Wikidata): potato, barley

Fun facts:

- (culture) Mulgipuder is the only Estonian national food that is in the UNESCO List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity.
- (origin) Mulgipuder comes from Estonia.

### Kama (food)

Kama (from Estonian) is a dish from the Baltic region, most commonly eaten in Estonia and Finland (where it is called talkkuna), based on milled cereals and legumes, traditionally prepared and eaten uncooked as fast food by combining the fine flour mixture with just cow milk or buttermilk.

- From: Estonia
- Origin (Wikidata): Finland, Russia, Belarus, Estonia, Ukraine, Armenia

Fun facts:

- (origin) Historically, kama was a farmer dish; it originated from southern Estonia, and it was made slightly differently in each geographic area; it could consist of oats, peas, beans or wheat, which were mixed together depending on the region.
- (about) Nowadays, Estonians kama remains popular and many Estonians consider it a distinctive "national food".
- (origin) Kama comes from Finland, Russia, Belarus, Estonia, Ukraine, Armenia.

More fun facts (when the user asks for more):

- (about) In Finland, talkkunajauho is made by first steaming grains, then grinding them up and finally roasting them.
