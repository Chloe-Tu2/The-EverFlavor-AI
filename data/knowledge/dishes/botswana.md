### Bogobe jwa lerotse

Bogobe jwa lerotse is a type of porridge eaten in Botswana and also a national dish of Botswana, characterized by a delicate flavor imparted by the lerotse melon, a variety of citron melon (citrullus lanatus var. citroides).

- From: Botswana
- Origin (Wikidata): Botswana
- Ingredients (Wikidata): milk, melon, sorghum grain

Fun facts:

- (culture) Bogobe jwa lerotse is typically stirred with a traditional wooden whisk, called a lehetho, and has a porridge-like consistency when fully cooked.
- (origin) Bogobe jwa lerotse comes from Botswana.

### Delele

Delele is a Zimbabwean, Zambian, north-eastern Botswana and Northern South African dish made from a local plant of the same name, and often eaten with sadza or phaletšhe or Vhuswa.

### Dikgobe

Dikgobe, also known as izinkobe, is a South African word for samp and beans cooked together.

- From: Botswana
- Origin (Wikidata): Botswana
- Ingredients (Wikidata): bean, samp

Fun facts:

- (culture) Dikgobe is commonly served at Setswana celebrations of life, such as marriage, and those marking death, the passage out of life.
- (origin) Dikgobe comes from Botswana.

### Seswaa

Seswaa (as the dish is called in the north of Botswana) or loswao (as the dish is called in the south of the country and western South Africa) is a traditional meat dish of Botswana, made of beef or goat meat.

- From: Botswana
- Origin (Wikidata): Botswana

Fun facts:

- (culture) Seswaa is normally prepared for ceremonies such as funerals, weddings and national events such as independence celebrations.
- (origin) Seswaa comes from Botswana.
