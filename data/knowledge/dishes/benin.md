### Amiwo

Amiwo is a traditional dish in Benin, consisting of a savory pudding made from spices (onions, garlic, black pepper, salt), cornflour and tomato paste, cooking oil or more traditionally palm oil.

- From: Benin
- Origin (Wikidata): Benin
- Ingredients (Wikidata): palm oil, tomato paste, onion, garlic, maize flour

Fun facts:

- (origin) Amiwo comes from Benin.

### Akpan (dessert)

Akpan, also known as akassa, is a fermented maize yogurt.

- From: Benin
- Origin (Wikidata): Togo, Benin
- Ingredients (Wikidata): milk, maize

Fun facts:

- (origin) Akpan comes from Togo, Benin.
