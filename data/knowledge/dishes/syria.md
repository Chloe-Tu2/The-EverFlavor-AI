### Shʿifurah

Shaʿifūra or jaʿifūra is a Syrian salad consisting of shanklish cheese with chopped tomato, onion and olive oil.

- From: Syria
- Origin (Wikidata): Syria
- Ingredients (Wikidata): tomato, olive oil, Shanklish, onion

Fun facts:

- (about) Shaʿifūra salad has a popular origin, and in each home it is prepared in a different way.
- (origin) Shʿifurah comes from Syria.
- (about) Shʿifurah is also known as bāzarkān, a name possibly of Kurdish origin, and is sometimes called simply shanklish salad.
