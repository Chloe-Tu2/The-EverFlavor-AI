### Bourek (Algerian cuisine)

Algerian bourek, also known as "brik" in eastern Algeria, is a popular variation of the savory pastry burek found in the Middle East and North Africa.

- From: Algeria
- Origin (Wikidata): Algeria
- Ingredients (Wikidata): cheese, meat, pastry

Fun facts:

- (about) Algerian bourek is usually made up of thin pastry sheets, known as "dyoul" in Algeria, which can be purchased from stores or made at home.
- (culture) Algerian bourek is often served as an appetizer or snack, and can be found in many street food vendors and restaurants throughout the country.
- (origin) Bourek comes from Algeria.

### Hmiss

Hmiss or ifelfel, meaning "chilli pepper" in Kabylia, or felfla and chlita in the region of Oran, is a traditional Algerian salad made from grilled peppers and tomatoes, chopped, mixed and seasoned with olive oil.

- From: Algeria
- Origin (Wikidata): Algeria

Fun facts:

- (history) In 1975, French chef and author Marcell Boulestin labeled hmiss in his 'Boulestin's Round-the-year Cookbook' simply as the Algerian salad.
- (origin) Hmiss comes from Algeria.

### Mahdjouba

Mahdjouba or mhadjeb is a crepe-like semolina based flatbread originating from Algeria.

- From: Algeria
- Origin (Wikidata): Algeria
- Ingredients (Wikidata): flour, semolina, onion, tomato

Fun facts:

- (origin) The mahdjouba, which means "covered" or "veiled" in Algerian Darja, originates from southern Algeria: Biskra, Touggourt.
- (culture) A traditional Algerian dish, mhadjeb is a fine crepe-like semolina-based flatbread, typically stuffed with a mixture of onion, garlic, tomato, peppers and spices.
- (about) Mahdjouba is very popular in all the regions of Algeria, including the southern regions such as Ouargla, Ghardaia, and Tamanrasset.

More fun facts (when the user asks for more):

- (culture) Mahdjouba is one of the essential dishes offered in Algerian street foods.
- (culture) Mahdjouba is the vegetable-filled variation of m'semen, a traditional North African flatbread.
- (origin) Mahdjouba comes from Algeria.
