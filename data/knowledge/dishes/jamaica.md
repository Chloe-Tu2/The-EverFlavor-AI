### Black Forest gateau

- From: Jamaica
- Origin (Wikidata): Germany
- Named after: Black Forest
- Ingredients (Wikidata): cherry, chocolate cake, kirsch, whipped cream

Fun facts:

- (history) However, the cake's recipe from 1927 is kept at an archive in Radolfzell, Germany.
