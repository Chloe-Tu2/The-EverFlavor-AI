### Catatos

Catatos is a traditional Angolan dish made with fried caterpillars and garlic.

- From: Angola

Fun facts:

- (culture) Catatos, a traditional Uíge dish, is made by frying caterpillars with garlic.
- (culture) The consumption of insects is an ancient indigenous tradition in many parts of the world, including southern Africa, due to their high nutritious value.
- (culture) Other caterpillar species traditionally eaten in Angola include the larvae of the Imbrasia epimethea, Imbrasia ertli and Usta terpsichore.

More fun facts (when the user asks for more):

- (culture) Traditional methods of gathering and preparation vary depending on the species.

### Moambe chicken

Moambe chicken is a savory chicken dish popular in Central Africa and considered the national dish of Angola, the Republic of the Congo, and the Democratic Republic of the Congo.

- From: Angola

### Cocada amarela

Cocada Amarela is a traditional Angolan dessert made from eggs and coconut.

- From: Angola
- Origin (Wikidata): Angola
- Ingredients (Wikidata): egg as food

Fun facts:

- (culture) Due to Angola's colonial history, Cocada Amarela is highly influenced by Portuguese pastries, which are known for their large quantities of egg yellow in traditional recipes.
- (origin) Cocada amarela comes from Angola.
