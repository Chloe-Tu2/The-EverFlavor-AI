### Boeber

- From: South Africa
- Origin (Wikidata): South Africa

### Chakalaka

Chakalaka is a South African vegetable relish, usually spicy, that is traditionally served with bread, pap, samp, stews, or curries.

- From: South Africa
- Origin (Wikidata): South Africa
- Ingredients (Wikidata): vegetable, bean, onion, tomato

Fun facts:

- (origin) Chakalaka is said to have originated in the townships of Johannesburg or in the gold mines surrounding Johannesburg, when Mozambican mineworkers leaving their shift cooked tinned produce (tomatoes, beans) with chili to produce a spicy Portuguese-style relish to accompany pap.
- (culture) Many variations of Chakalaka exist, depending on region and family tradition.
- (culture) Chakalaka is also served at eateries and supermarket delis that have traditional South African or Botswana menu.
