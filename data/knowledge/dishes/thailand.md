### Cendol

- From: Thailand
- Origin (Wikidata): Indonesia, Java
- Ingredients (Wikidata): table salt, ice, coconut milk, rice flour, palm sugar, jackfruit, glutinous rice flour, sago flour

### Kai yang

Fun facts:

- (culture) The town of Seno in Savannakhet, Laos is renowned for its flavorful and tender Ping kai Xeno or Xeno grilled chicken; the dish has earned official recognition as Savannakhet's symbol and culinary legacy.
- (about) In Thailand, there are also many famous Thai Muslim varieties of kai yang which are not of Lao origin at all, but more akin to the grilled chicken from Malaysia.
- (origin) Kai yang comes from Laos.
