### Makmoura

Makmoura is a traditional Levantine dish associated with the Hauran region, which includes southern Syria as well as the rural areas of northern Jordan, including Ar-Ramtha, Huwwarah, and the villages surrounding Irbid.

- From: Jordan
- Origin (Wikidata): Jordan

Fun facts:

- (culture) This dish is commonly prepared for celebrations, family gatherings, and local festivals, and it has become an integral part of Irbid’s cultural identity.
- (origin) Makmoura comes from Jordan.
- (culture) Makmoura was traditionally made to celebrate the olive harvest season, as it uses olive oil as a primary ingredient.
